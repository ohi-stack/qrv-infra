# OneGodian Remember Campaign — QRV Integration Plan

## Purpose

The OneGodian: Remember Campaign should be treated as a public campaign asset that can be verified through QRV.network.

This allows every business card, flyer, product label, contribution page, QR code, YouTube link, and campaign post to point back to one verifiable public record.

## Recommended QRV Record

```txt
QRVID: QRV-ONEGODIAN-REMEMBER-000001
Record Type: campaign
Issuer: ONEGODIAN, LLC
Title: OneGodian: Remember Campaign
Canonical Page: https://onegodian.org/remember
Verification Page: https://verify.qrv.network/QRV-ONEGODIAN-REMEMBER-000001
Status: VERIFIED
```

## Add to WordPress Page

Add a verification section after the hero CTA block and before the YouTube video block.

### Suggested Heading

```txt
Verify This Campaign
```

### Suggested Copy

```txt
The OneGodian: Remember Campaign is connected to a QRV.network verification record so visitors can confirm the official campaign page, support links, public message, and campaign identity before contributing, shopping, sharing, or subscribing.
```

### Suggested CTA Buttons

```txt
Verify Campaign Record → https://verify.qrv.network/QRV-ONEGODIAN-REMEMBER-000001
Visit QRV.network → https://qrv.network
```

## Recommended Page Placement

Insert the QRV verification block in this order:

1. Hero message
2. Three campaign meaning cards
3. Main support/shop CTA row
4. QRV verification block
5. YouTube video
6. What the Remember Campaign Means
7. Support actions
8. Campaign phrases
9. Final CTA
10. Public educational disclaimer

## QRV Use Cases From This Page

- Campaign authenticity verification
- Product QR labels for Remember merchandise
- Contribution QR code verification
- YouTube campaign QR verification
- Business card scan verification
- Public support record verification
- Documented campaign origin and official message

## WordPress Production Notes

- Keep contribution language voluntary and public-safe.
- Avoid unsupported investment, guaranteed return, or securities language.
- Use ONEGODIAN, LLC for commercial/product/support activity.
- Use OneGodian.org for public education, identity, remembrance, and campaign explanation.
- QRV.network should verify record authenticity, not make legal or financial claims.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Campaign page has a visible QRV verification block.
- QRVID resolves publicly on verify.qrv.network.
- The verification record identifies the official campaign title, issuer, canonical URL, and current status.
- The QRV block links back to QRV.network for public trust explanation.
- Support, shop, YouTube, and contribution links remain separate CTAs.
